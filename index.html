    Why it’s free

    A score in a drawer doesn’t do anything. So I’m putting nearly everything I’ve written online, for anyone to use. There’s nothing to buy here and no form to fill out. Download the PDF and go.

    The scores here are released under [Creative Commons license: not chosen yet]. Using the music explains what that lets you do, how royalties work, and how to credit me.

    Lately

    A thread I’ve noticed in my recent work: music that steps outside the accepted ideas of what a piece, a genre or a performance is. My doctoral dissertation was a piece that can’t physically be performed as written. The next big piece after it was a chamber work that isn’t meant to be listened to.
