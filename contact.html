Contact

Just write to me.

There’s no form. Email is the way to reach me.

me@matthewsamson.com

Good reasons to write

  • You performed or recorded something. I’d really like to hear how it went, and to see a program or a link if there is one.
  • You found a mistake in a score. Tell me the piece, the page and the bar, and I’ll fix the PDF for everyone.
  • You made an arrangement. I’d love to see it.
  • You’re not sure the license covers what you want to do. Ask. More on that here.
  • You’re looking for a piece that isn’t on the site yet. Plenty of older music is still on its way. Ask and I’ll tell you where it stands.
